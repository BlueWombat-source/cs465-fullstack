# cs465-fullstack
CS-465 Full Stack Development with MEAN

Reflection
This course has had a meaningful impact on my professional goals and has even shifted the direction I see myself pursuing within computer science. Coming into the program, I was primarily interested in game development or cybersecurity. However, working through the full stack project, building both the customer‑facing site and the secure administrative SPA, showed me how much I enjoy the problem‑solving, architecture planning, and hands‑on development involved in full stack work. Completing the Travlr Getaways application gave me a clearer picture of what modern web development looks like in practice, and it made me seriously consider pursuing full stack development as a career path.

Throughout the course, I strengthened several skills that will make me a more marketable candidate in the field. I learned how to design and implement RESTful APIs, work with NoSQL databases, and build dynamic client‑side interfaces using Angular. I also gained experience with authentication, routing, component lifecycle management, and debugging across both the frontend and backend layers. Beyond the technical skills, this course helped me develop a deeper understanding of how different parts of a full stack application communicate and depend on one another, which is essential for building scalable and maintainable systems.

Overall, this project gave me confidence in my ability to work across the entire stack and helped me discover a new area of computer science that I genuinely enjoy. The skills I developed, ranging from API design to SPA architecture, will continue to support my growth as I move forward in my degree and prepare for a career in full stack development.
